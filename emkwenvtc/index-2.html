Welcome to Emkwen Vocational Centre

The centre offers training programmes up to ARTISAN level providing opportunities for youth and adults to acquire marketable skills in various technical and vocational fields.

Emkwen Vocational Centre is fully licensed with TVETA and is an examination centre for trade courses examined by NITA, KNEC, and CIDACC.
